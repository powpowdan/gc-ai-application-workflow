# OBSERVED SCREENING QUESTIONS: question-format archive

A running catalog of REAL question formats encountered in GC posters.
Mine it for format and phrasing, never for claims. When a process closes,
paste its questions in. Your own strongest answers may be appended as
format reference, marked [ANSWERED YYYY-MM]; claims themselves live in
EVIDENCE-MAP and in the POSTINGS/ answer files. EVIDENCE-MAP wins any
conflict with older material.

Patterns to notice in the formats below: yes/no experience openers,
"recent experience" qualifiers, combined-verb qualifications ("installing,
configuring and maintaining"), multi-site or environment qualifiers, and
asset questions phrased as optional.

## Observed formats (from real GC IT posters)

### Yes/no experience (classic openers)

- Do you have experience providing IT recommendations or solutions to a
  client?
- Do you have experience contributing to IM/IT projects or initiatives?
- Do you have experience working with modern software development
  architectures?
- Do you have experience in using .NET, SQL or XML, or Java or JavaScript?
- Do you have experience in the use of relational database systems?
- Do you have experience working with Cloud technologies in the support or
  applications development?
- Do you have experience supporting the development of technical standards?
- Do you have experience coordinating or facilitating working groups?
- Do you have experience with version control practices and techniques?
- Do you have experience in security and access control in applications?
- Do you have experience designing and developing reports or dashboards to
  meet organizational reporting related requirements?

### "Recent experience" + combined verbs

- Do you have recent experience providing client support for IT systems in
  a multi-site environment?
- Do you have recent experience installing, configuring and maintaining
  server based IT applications?

### Advice, documentation, administration

- Experience providing technical advice and guidance to management in the
  delivery of IT solutions.
- Experience in M365 administration.
- Do you have experience producing documentation and end user
  correspondence?

## Answer-format conventions observed

- Opening sentence mirrors the question verbatim: "Yes, I have experience
  [mirrored wording] in my position as [title] ([classification]) for
  [department] ([dates])."
- Numbered-positions header when experience spans positions; some posters
  MANDATE a structure (e.g., Organization / Position & Duration / Details
  per criterion)
- Date format can be mandated by the application FORM rather than the
  poster (e.g., "M-Y to M-Y"): always copy from the live form before
  drafting
